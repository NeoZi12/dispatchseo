## MarketMuse's own line: "we do not write content for our customers"

MarketMuse's real capability is deeper than a single term-coverage score - checked live against marketmuse.com while writing this, the product runs on proprietary topic modeling (Personalized Difficulty, Topic Authority, Competitive Advantage, Content Score, Page Authority), a Content Inventory that tracks every page against those metrics automatically, and a Topic Navigator for keyword and cluster research. The Optimize module can generate a content brief in up to nine formats on its top tier - article, comparison, FAQ collection, guide, how-to, listicle, local, news/event, product review - built from the same competitive analysis. None of that is thin. What it isn't is a publishing tool, and the homepage doesn't hedge on that: the generative-AI component helps a human draft faster, it doesn't draft or ship on its own.

## What the four tiers actually unlock

Free gives one user ten queries a month and nothing else - no tracked topics, no briefs, no site inventory. Optimize is the first paid step: one site inventory, 100 tracked topics, five article-only briefs a month. Research triples the topic ceiling to 1,000 and adds two more users and unlimited queries, still capped at ten briefs and article-only format. Strategy is the only tier that unlocks all nine brief types, five users, and 10,000 tracked topics. None of the three paid tiers list a price - MarketMuse's checkout ends at "request a demo," which puts it in the same quote-based bucket [this site already flagged when it covered Surfer's shortlist](/blog/surfer-seo-alternative). The gating logic is consistent throughout: more topics tracked, more briefs generated, more formats available - never a publish button.

## Where a shared topic model and a live brief still win

None of this makes MarketMuse's actual product pointless. If a team of writers is drafting content by hand regardless, and what they need is Topic Navigator's cluster research plus a shared brief the whole team can open and edit against the same Content Score, that's a real, different job - the one Research and Strategy are priced and seated for, five users and a dedicated content inventory included. DispatchSEO has no equivalent screen: there's no live topic model to watch update as a writer works, no proprietary difficulty score, no shared workspace for a second person to open. The review surface here is a pull request diff, and after that, whatever the rank and citation crons report back on their own schedule.

## When not to use this

Stay on MarketMuse, Clearscope, or Frase if a human writer - you, a hire, a freelancer - is doing the drafting either way and what you want is a topic model and a brief to draft against, or if a team needs to collaborate on the same content inventory at once. DispatchSEO needs its own DataForSEO account behind the research and a coding agent already working in your repo; there's no drop-in brief to hand a human writer, because the point is that no human writer is in the loop. If the narrower question is just whether a page you're about to write already overlaps one you shipped last year, [a free keyword clustering pass](/free-tools/keyword-clustering-tool) answers that without a MarketMuse subscription or a DispatchSEO account either.

## FAQ

**Does MarketMuse write or publish content?**
No - checked live on its own homepage, MarketMuse states directly it doesn't write content for customers. Its Optimize module has generative-AI assistance to help a human draft faster, and nothing in any of its four tiers publishes to a CMS or website.

**How much does MarketMuse cost?**
Only the Free tier has a listed price ($0, ten queries a month, no tracked topics or briefs). Optimize, Research, and Strategy are all quote-based - MarketMuse's pricing page ends at a demo request, not a checkout, for every paid tier.

**What's the actual difference between MarketMuse's tiers?**
Tracked topics, briefs per month, and brief format variety - Optimize caps out at 100 topics and five article-only briefs, Research triples the topic ceiling with unlimited queries, and only Strategy unlocks all nine brief formats and the full 10,000-topic ceiling.

**Is there a free MarketMuse alternative?**
MarketMuse's own Free tier is the free option inside its own product, capped at ten queries a month with no briefs or tracked topics. Past that, this project's own AI-citation check found Capterra, Alex Birkett's blog, and five other sites naming themselves as free alternatives when asked directly - worth checking against their current claims rather than taking any one roundup's word for it.

**Does DispatchSEO replace MarketMuse's topic modeling?**
Not the proprietary metrics - Personalized Difficulty and Topic Authority are MarketMuse's own scoring, not something this project recalculates. What DispatchSEO replaces is the step after the brief: researching the gap, writing the full page, and shipping it as a reviewed pull request instead of a document someone still has to act on.

Every name holding today's page 1 for "marketmuse alternative," MarketMuse itself included, answers "what should this page cover" and stops there. That's a real, useful product category if a person is writing the page regardless - it's just not the same question as "who researches, writes, and ships it," and nothing on that page 1 names the option that finishes the job DispatchSEO starts the exact same way.
