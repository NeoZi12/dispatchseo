## What SEO automation software is actually supposed to do

Strip the marketing and "SEO automation software" collapses into four jobs, and most vendors cover some subset of them:

- **Technical audits and crawling** - broken links, missing metadata, schema errors, crawl budget waste.
- **Rank tracking and reporting** - SERP position over time, usually rolled into a client-facing dashboard.
- **[Content automation](/blog/surfer-seo-alternative)** - keyword research through a drafted (sometimes published) page, the part with the widest quality range.
- **All-in-one platforms** - a bundle of the above behind one login, priced per seat.

Page 1 for this query is dominated by the fourth category: platforms and the roundups that compare them. That's a reasonable answer if you're choosing software for a team - it's a much thinner answer if you're the one person doing the work.
