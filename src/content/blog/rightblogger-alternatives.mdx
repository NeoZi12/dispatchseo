If you are leaving RightBlogger over price, Autoblogging.ai or DispatchSEO start at $19 a month. If you are leaving because the free tier never showed you automated output, DispatchSEO publishes two articles free. If you need WordPress or Shopify, SEObot, Outrank, Autoblogging.ai and Arvow all publish there. If you liked the Git publishing, DispatchSEO is the only other tool that opens pull requests. Those are the five alternatives below, with prices read from their own pages on 2026-11-09. One of them is ours.

> **TL;DR** - RightBlogger's free plan writes 2,000 words a month and does not automate; Solo is $49 a month billed yearly for one site; publishing covers Webflow, Ghost and Duda but not WordPress or Shopify. Outrank and SEObot fix the WordPress gap at $99 and from $49. Autoblogging.ai and DispatchSEO fix the price at $19. DispatchSEO alone fixes the free-plan gap and keeps the Git publishing.

## Which alternative is built for volume?

Outrank. One plan at $99 a month, or $999 a year, for 30 articles a month with keyword research, AI images and YouTube embeds, published automatically. Add-ons take it to 60 or 90 articles, and the $1,399 a month Human Curated service adds editors who review and fact-check. It is the closest to RightBlogger Solo on WordPress, at about double the price, with no free plan and only watermarked trial articles.

## Which one asks for the least attention?

SEObot. It starts at $49 a month, describes itself as 100% autopilot by default, and offers an email to approve or decline each article. It publishes almost everywhere, including Framer, Notion and HubSpot, and claims fact-checking with cited sources, images and internal links. Its pricing page shows only the starting price. No free trial.

## Which one is cheapest to start?

Autoblogging.ai, tied with DispatchSEO. Starter is $19 a month ($12 billed yearly) for 40 credits on one site. A plain article is one credit and SERP analysis, images and internal links are one credit each, so a finished article is about four credits, roughly ten a month on Starter. A fact checker is listed on paid plans, and you can review before publishing. The free tier is 10 credits a month with no card. It also posts to Medium, Dev.to and Hashnode.

## Which one gives the most articles per dollar?

Arvow. Solo shows $39 for 1,000 credits on one site, Business $69 for 3,000 credits on five sites. An article costs 15 credits, so Solo is about 66 articles. The prices appear to be yearly rates (the page says "saves you $360 with yearly" and shows no monthly figure). No free trial, no approval step described, and fact-checking is not mentioned.

## Which one keeps the Git publishing?

DispatchSEO. It commits the article on a branch, opens a pull request, and merges it when the site is on Auto; with no repo it hosts the blog, and it also publishes to self-hosted WordPress. Every site gets two fact-checked articles a month free, with no card and a small badge on free articles, published on topics with measured search demand, with your Google stats in the dashboard. Paid plans are $19, $39 and $69 a month for one, three and five articles a week. It does not support Wix, Squarespace, Shopify or Webflow and has no writing assistant. The code is open source (AGPL-3.0). The two are compared in detail in [DispatchSEO vs RightBlogger](/blog/dispatchseo-vs-rightblogger).

## FAQ

**Is there an alternative with a free plan that publishes?** Two: DispatchSEO publishes finished articles, Autoblogging.ai publishes plain ones from 10 monthly credits. [Free auto-blogging tools](/blog/free-auto-blogging-tools) goes through the free tiers one by one.

**Which alternatives let me approve before publishing?** Autoblogging.ai (review or auto-post), SEObot (an approve-or-decline email), DispatchSEO on a repo site that is not on Auto (the pull request waits). Outrank's base plan and Arvow publish automatically.
