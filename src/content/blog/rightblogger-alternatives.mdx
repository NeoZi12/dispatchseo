<Verdict>

The best RightBlogger alternative depends on why you are leaving. For a lower price, Autoblogging.ai and DispatchSEO both start at $19 a month. For a free plan that actually publishes, DispatchSEO publishes two articles a month free, for sites that live in a GitHub repo. For the most articles per dollar, Arvow. For a hands-off 30 articles a month on WordPress, Outrank or SEObot. If you liked RightBlogger's Git pull requests, DispatchSEO is the only other tool here that opens them.

</Verdict>

## FAQ

<Faq>
<Q q="Does RightBlogger publish to WordPress?">

Yes. RightBlogger's Git page lists WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Framer, Ghost, Wix, Duda, Notion and more as places it publishes, alongside GitHub pull requests. Switching away is about price, the free plan or fact-checking, not platform support.

</Q>
<Q q="Is there an alternative with a free plan that publishes?">

DispatchSEO publishes two finished articles a month free. Autoblogging.ai's 10 free credits publish plain articles through your own WordPress connection. [Free auto-blogging tools](/blog/free-auto-blogging-tools) goes through every free tier.

</Q>
<Q q="Which alternatives let me approve before publishing?">

Autoblogging.ai (review or auto-post), SEObot (an approve-or-decline email) and DispatchSEO with Manual review switched on, where each article waits for you to press Publish. Outrank's base plan and Arvow publish automatically.

</Q>
<Q q="How does DispatchSEO compare with RightBlogger head to head?">

[DispatchSEO vs RightBlogger](/blog/dispatchseo-vs-rightblogger) covers price, Git publishing, fact-checking and where each one is better.

</Q>
</Faq>
