People look for a RightBlogger alternative for three reasons: the free plan writes 2,000 words a month and does not automate anything, the Solo plan is $49 a month billed yearly ($59 monthly) for one site, and the publishing list skips WordPress and Shopify in favour of Webflow, Ghost and Duda. Below are five tools that cover those gaps in different ways, with prices read from their own pages on 2026-11-09. One of them is ours.

> **TL;DR** - Cheaper entry: Autoblogging.ai at $19 a month or DispatchSEO at $19. A free plan that publishes: DispatchSEO (2 articles a month). WordPress and Shopify: SEObot, Outrank, Autoblogging.ai or Arvow. Thirty posts a month: Outrank ($99) or SEObot. A GitHub repo like RightBlogger's Git integration: only DispatchSEO. Fact-checking in the pipeline: SEObot, Autoblogging.ai, DispatchSEO.

## Outrank: the volume alternative

Outrank's one plan is $99 a month, or $999 a year, for 30 articles a month with keyword research, AI images and YouTube embeds, published automatically. Add-ons take it to 60 or 90 articles. The $1,399 a month Human Curated service adds editors who review and fact-check. It is the closest to "RightBlogger Solo, but on WordPress", at double the price and with no free tier.

## SEObot: the hands-off alternative

SEObot starts at $49 a month and describes itself as 100% autopilot by default, with an optional email to approve or decline each article. It publishes almost everywhere, including Framer, Notion and HubSpot, and claims fact-checking with cited sources, images and internal links. Its pricing page shows only the starting price; the tier table is not published there. No free trial.

## Autoblogging.ai: the cheap-entry alternative

The Starter plan is $19 a month ($12 billed yearly) for 40 credits on one site. A plain article is one credit; SERP analysis, images and internal links are one credit each, so a finished article is about four credits, which puts Starter at roughly ten full articles a month. A fact checker runs on paid plans, and you can review before publishing or let it post. The free tier gives 10 credits a month with no card. It also posts to Medium, Dev.to and Hashnode, which none of the others do.

## Arvow: the most articles per dollar

Arvow's Solo plan shows $39 for 1,000 credits on one site, Business $69 for 3,000 credits on five sites. An article costs 15 credits, so Solo is about 66 articles. The prices appear to be yearly rates (the page says "saves you $360 with yearly" and shows no monthly figure). There is no free trial, no approval step is described, and fact-checking is not mentioned. It is the pick if you want quantity and will do your own checking.

## DispatchSEO: the free-to-start, Git-friendly alternative

DispatchSEO is the only alternative here that also publishes to a GitHub repository. The server commits the article on a branch, opens a pull request, and merges it when the site is on Auto; if the site has no repo, it hosts the blog instead. Every site gets two fact-checked articles a month free, with no card and no end date, published on topics with measured search demand, with the Google clicks shown per article. Paid plans are $19, $39 and $69 a month for one, three and five articles a week. It does not support Wix, Squarespace, Shopify or Webflow, and it has no writing assistant for your own drafts. It is open source (AGPL-3.0).

## Matching the alternative to the reason you are leaving

| If you are leaving because... | Try |
|---|---|
| The free plan never showed me automated output | DispatchSEO (two published articles free) or Autoblogging.ai (10 free credits, plain drafts) |
| $49 a month is too much for one site | Autoblogging.ai or DispatchSEO at $19 |
| My site is on WordPress or Shopify | SEObot, Outrank, Autoblogging.ai or Arvow |
| I need 30 or more posts a month | Outrank, or SEObot's higher tiers |
| I liked the Git publishing | DispatchSEO, the only other tool that opens pull requests |
| I want someone to check the facts | SEObot, Autoblogging.ai or DispatchSEO in the pipeline; Outrank only on the human-edited tier |
