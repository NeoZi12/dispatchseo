<Verdict>

Two free auto-blogging plans publish an article to your site: DispatchSEO, with two fact-checked articles a month and a small badge, for sites that live in a GitHub repo, and Autoblogging.ai, with 10 credits a month for plain articles through your own WordPress connection. RightBlogger's free tier writes 2,000 words but does not publish, Outrank's trial articles are watermarked, and SEObot and Arvow have no free plan.

</Verdict>

## What should "publishes for you" mean?

A free plan that hands you 2,000 words is a free writing assistant, which is useful but is not auto-blogging. The test on this page: on the free plan, with no card, does an article end up live on a site you own without you pasting anything? DispatchSEO and Autoblogging.ai pass. Autoblogging.ai comes with a caveat: ten credits is ten plain articles or two or three with images, analysis and links, the free tier leaves out its fact checker, and you connect the destination yourself. DispatchSEO's two articles arrive finished and fact-checked, but only on a site that lives in a GitHub repo.

## What is the catch on each free plan?

- **DispatchSEO:** the same product at a slower pace, for sites in a GitHub repo only. Every free article carries a small DispatchSEO badge, which the paid plans remove. There is no card and no end date.
- **Autoblogging.ai:** ten credits are a sample of its credit system, without the fact checker.
- **RightBlogger:** the editor without the automation, so you see the writing but not the autopilot.
- **Outrank:** you see real articles, but with a watermark until you subscribe.
- **SEObot and Arvow:** no free tier; SEObot offers a refund after the first article instead.

## When is free enough?

Two articles a month is a real cadence for a side project or a new SaaS in its first quarter, when the job is a dozen indexed pages and a Search Console you actually read; [SEO for SaaS](/blog/seo-for-saas) lays out that first year. It is not a cadence for a site competing on volume. If you need a post a day, no free plan gets you there; the cheapest paid routes are RightBlogger Solo at $49 billed yearly and Arvow Solo at $39 billed yearly, both covered in [best AI auto-blogging tools](/blog/best-ai-auto-blogging-tools).

A free plan is also the only way to judge quality on your own topics. Sample articles on a vendor's site were written on topics that show the tool off; a free plan writes about yours.

## FAQ

<Faq>
<Q q="Does the free DispatchSEO plan need a GitHub repo?">

Yes. DispatchSEO works with websites that live in a GitHub repo (Next.js, Astro, Hugo, anything you deploy from code), plus a free Google Search Console account. WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow and Ghost are not supported. Support for Lovable sites is coming soon.

</Q>
<Q q="Will two free articles a month hurt my site?">

Google's spam rules target bulk pages made to manipulate rankings. Two fact-checked articles a month on topics people search for is the opposite of bulk.

</Q>
<Q q="Can I remove the badge without paying?">

No. The badge is the price of the free plan. Starter at $19 a month removes it and raises the pace to 5 articles a month.

</Q>
</Faq>
