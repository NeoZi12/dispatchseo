<Verdict>

The best AI auto-blogging tool depends on where your blog lives and how many articles you want. For about 30 articles a month on WordPress, RightBlogger, Outrank or SEObot. For the cheapest paid start, Autoblogging.ai at $19. For the most articles per dollar, Arvow. For a blog in a GitHub repo, RightBlogger or DispatchSEO, and to see finished articles before paying anything, DispatchSEO is the only one of the six whose free plan publishes them with every claim checked. DispatchSEO works only with sites that live in a GitHub repo.

</Verdict>

## Which ones publish on autopilot, and which let you look first?

SEObot calls itself 100% autopilot by default and offers an approve-or-decline email. Outrank's base plan and Arvow publish automatically. Autoblogging.ai lets you review before publishing or auto-post. RightBlogger opens a pull request on Git by default, which waits for your merge. DispatchSEO runs on Autopilot by default: each article goes live once it is written and checked. With Manual review switched on, each one waits for you to press Publish.

## What about sites built from code?

Two of the six document publishing into a Git repository. RightBlogger commits each post and its images and opens a pull request by default, for Markdown, MDX or JSON-file blogs on GitHub or Cloudflare Artifacts. DispatchSEO connects to a GitHub repo, adds each article as a pull request, and on Autopilot takes it live on its own. SEObot lists Next.js among its integrations without saying how. GitHub repos are the only kind of site DispatchSEO works with: it does not support WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow or Ghost, and support for Lovable sites is coming soon.

## How do I choose?

Start from two questions: where does your blog live, and how many articles a month do you want? About 30 a month on WordPress points to RightBlogger, Outrank or SEObot. A few a month on a budget points to Autoblogging.ai. If you want volume above all and will edit the posts yourself, Arvow. A blog in a Git repo narrows it to RightBlogger or DispatchSEO, compared in [DispatchSEO vs RightBlogger](/blog/dispatchseo-vs-rightblogger). If you are not sure yet, start with a free plan that publishes; [free auto-blogging tools](/blog/free-auto-blogging-tools) lists them.

## FAQ

<Faq>
<Q q="Is auto-blogging safe for Google rankings?">

Google's spam policy targets pages produced in bulk to manipulate rankings, however they were made. Thirty unchecked articles a month on guessed topics carry that risk; a few checked articles on topics with real demand carry much less. The risk grows with volume, whichever tool writes the articles.

</Q>
<Q q="Which of these is open source?">

DispatchSEO (AGPL-3.0), which can also be self-hosted. None of the others list public source code on their sites.

</Q>
<Q q="What does one article cost on each tool?">

From about 59 cents to about $3.80, depending on how many articles the plan includes and whether anything checks them. [What an AI-written SEO article costs in 2026](/blog/what-an-ai-seo-article-costs) works it out per tool.

</Q>
