Six tools will write and publish blog articles for you without a writer in the loop. They differ on three things that matter more than the model behind them: where the article can land (WordPress and Webflow are universal, a Git repository is rare), whether anything checks the facts before publishing, and what a published article costs you per month. This page is written by the maker of one of the six, so read the DispatchSEO row with that in mind. Every price below was read from the vendor's own pricing page on 2026-11-09; where a vendor does not publish a number, the table says so instead of guessing.

> **TL;DR** - Outrank and RightBlogger are the volume plays (30 articles a month from $83 to $99). Autoblogging.ai is the cheapest paid entry ($19 a month, about 10 full articles). SEObot is the most "set and forget" and publishes almost everywhere, from $49. Arvow is cheap per article but publishes with no review step and no fact check. DispatchSEO is the only one with a free plan that publishes finished articles (2 a month), the only one besides RightBlogger that lands articles in a GitHub repo, and it tops out at 5 a week. If you publish to WordPress and want 30 posts a month, pick Outrank or SEObot. If your site is built from code, or you want to see it work before paying, start with DispatchSEO.

## Which ones check facts before publishing?

This is the question that separates "auto-blogging" from "spam machine", and vendors are quiet about it.

- **SEObot** claims fact-checking with cited sources on its homepage.
- **Autoblogging.ai** lists a fact checker on all paid plans that verifies claims against live sources.
- **Outrank** mentions fact-checking only on its $1,399 a month Human Curated tier, where editors review each piece.
- **DispatchSEO** runs every draft through a citation check: each claim is judged against the passage of the source it cites, and an article that cannot be grounded in more than one source is parked rather than published.
- **RightBlogger** and **Arvow** do not mention fact-checking on the pages we read.

## Which ones publish on autopilot, and which ones let you look first?

SEObot describes itself as 100% autopilot by default, with an optional approve-or-decline email. Outrank's base plan publishes automatically. Arvow publishes and updates automatically with no approval step described. Autoblogging.ai lets you review before publishing or auto-post. RightBlogger keeps posts as drafts or, on Git, opens a pull request you merge. DispatchSEO publishes the moment an article passes its gate; on a GitHub site that means a pull request that merges itself when the site is on Auto, or waits for you when it is not, so the article's full history sits in your repo either way.

## What about sites built from code?

Most of these tools assume a CMS. If your blog is Markdown in a Next.js, Astro or Hugo repo, only two options publish into it. RightBlogger's Git integration commits the post and its images and opens a pull request by default, with a toggle to commit straight to the production branch. DispatchSEO does the same from its server, reads a post that is already in your repo and mirrors its frontmatter, and merges the pull request when the site is on Auto. If your site has no repo, DispatchSEO can host the blog for you instead; RightBlogger cannot.

## Where each tool wins

**RightBlogger** wins on breadth: a writing toolkit, keyword research, a post-publish "Site Agent" that audits and edits older posts, and the most mature Git publishing. It is the right pick for a solo blogger who wants one subscription for everything.

**SEObot** wins on integrations and on not having to touch anything. If you want 20 posts a month to appear on a Framer or Notion site with no dashboard visits, it is built for that.

**Outrank** wins on volume per dollar at the 30-a-month mark, and it is the only one offering a human-edited tier for people who want quantity without the quality risk.

**Autoblogging.ai** wins on price of entry and on publishing destinations beyond your own site (Medium, Dev.to, Hashnode). The credit arithmetic takes a minute to understand.

**Arvow** wins on raw articles per dollar, if you are comfortable with no review step and no fact check.

**DispatchSEO** wins when you want to see the output before paying, when your site lives in a repo, or when you would rather publish 1 to 5 fact-checked articles a week on topics with measured search demand than 30 on topics the tool guessed. It loses to every other tool here on volume, and it does not support Wix, Squarespace, Shopify or Webflow yet.

## How to choose in two questions

Where does your blog live, and how many articles do you want a month? WordPress and 30 a month: Outrank or SEObot. WordPress and a few a month: Autoblogging.ai. A Git repo: RightBlogger or DispatchSEO. Not sure yet: the DispatchSEO free plan publishes two real articles so you can judge the output on your own site, and Autoblogging.ai's 10 free credits get you a few plain drafts.

## Frequently asked

**Is auto-blogging safe for Google rankings?** Google's spam policy targets pages made in bulk to manipulate rankings, however they were produced. Tools that publish 30 unchecked articles a month on guessed topics carry that risk. Tools that pick topics with real demand and check facts carry less. Volume is the risk factor, not the AI.

**Do any of these tools write in languages other than English?** Autoblogging.ai lists 38 languages. DispatchSEO writes in the site's language, including Hebrew. The others were not checked for this page.

**Which one is open source?** DispatchSEO (AGPL-3.0), which can be self-hosted. None of the others publish their code.
