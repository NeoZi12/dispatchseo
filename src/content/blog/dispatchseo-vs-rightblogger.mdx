<Verdict>

RightBlogger is a writing suite with automation: $49 a month billed yearly buys one site and up to one automated post a day, and it publishes to a dozen platforms including WordPress, Webflow and a GitHub repo. DispatchSEO is a narrower SEO autopilot that works only with websites that live in a GitHub repo: two fact-checked articles a month free, then 5 to 30 a month for $19 to $69. Both open pull requests, and on DispatchSEO's default Autopilot setting each article also goes live on its own.

</Verdict>

DispatchSEO's paid plans also add weekly keyword research, keyword rankings and advanced analytics, and remove the badge.

## How does each one publish to a repo?

RightBlogger connects through its GitHub app or a pasted token, and also supports Cloudflare Artifacts repositories. It handles Markdown or MDX blogs with frontmatter and blogs built from a JSON content file (Astro, Next.js, Hugo, Eleventy, Jekyll). Each post opens as a pull request by default; a toggle commits straight to the production branch. It then checks that the change reached the live site and asks the host to rebuild if not.

DispatchSEO connects to a GitHub repo and a free Google Search Console account, and adds each article to the site as a pull request. On Autopilot, the default, the article goes live on its own once it is written and checked. With Manual review switched on, each article waits: you get an email, read it, change anything you like, and press Publish.

## What goes into each article?

RightBlogger's pricing page does not mention fact-checking or source citations in automated posts; its Site Agent suggests internal-link and SEO edits after publishing. On DispatchSEO, every topic comes from keyword research with real monthly searches behind it, and every claim in an article is checked against a source before it goes live. It keeps a steady pace, never two articles in one day.

## Where RightBlogger is better

- It works with far more sites: WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Webflow, Framer, Ghost, Duda and Notion as well as Git. DispatchSEO works only with sites that live in a GitHub repo.
- It is cheaper at volume: Solo's up to 30 posts a month cost $49 billed yearly; DispatchSEO's 30 a month cost $59 billed yearly on Scale.
- It helps with the posts you write yourself, because it is a full writing toolkit. DispatchSEO only publishes the articles it writes.
- Its Agency plan covers ten sites on one plan; DispatchSEO stops at five.
- It supports Cloudflare Artifacts repositories; DispatchSEO is GitHub only.

## Where DispatchSEO is better

- Its free plan publishes two finished articles a month on your own site, with no card and no end date. RightBlogger's free tier writes words but does not automate.
- Every claim is checked against a source before the article goes live.
- On Autopilot, articles go live without you merging anything; RightBlogger leaves the merge to you unless you commit straight to production.
- It costs less to start: $19 a month, or $15 billed yearly, against $49 billed yearly.
- Your Google stats sit in the same dashboard that publishes the articles.

## Can I try DispatchSEO without leaving RightBlogger?

Yes, if your site lives in a GitHub repo. DispatchSEO's free plan needs no card, so you can connect the repo, let it publish its two articles for the month, and compare them with what RightBlogger writes before changing anything.
