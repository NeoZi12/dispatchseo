## FAQ

**Is Google Search Console really free with no limits?**
Yes - it's free forever and has no lookup cap, because it only ever reports on a property you've already verified. The tradeoff is scope, not cost: zero visibility into anyone else's site.

**Does Keyword Planner show real numbers without running any ads?**
It shows volume ranges either way, but the interface itself is gated behind creating a Google Ads account first - free to open, but not a tool you can reach without one.

**What's the real free-tier cap on Screaming Frog?**
500 URLs per crawl, verified on screamingfrog.co.uk while writing this - past that, or past wanting to save a crawl for later, it asks for a license.

**Are Ahrefs', [Moz's](/blog/moz-alternative), and SE Ranking's "free tools" pages free forever?**
Yes, the individual calculators stay free - they're built as a lead-in to each vendor's paid platform, [not a limited trial with an expiration date](/blog/seo-automation-software-free). Expect a signup prompt at some point, not a paywall on the tool itself.

**What do I lose by never paying for an SEO tool?**
Mostly scale and convenience: no built-in scheduling, no cross-tool memory, and volume/difficulty data that's a range instead of an exact number. None of that blocks a single small site from doing real SEO work - it just means you're the integration layer, which is the one thing every free-tools roundup leaves for you to build yourself.

The tools on every "best free" list are exactly as free as advertised, and fine tools in their own right. What none of them do is remember, on their own, that you asked - and that's a wiring problem a sixth tab can't fix, whichever five you bookmark today.
