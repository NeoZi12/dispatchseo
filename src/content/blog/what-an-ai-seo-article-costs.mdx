<Verdict>

An AI-written SEO article costs between about 59 cents and $3.80 on the six auto-blogging tools we checked. Arvow is the cheapest per article, RightBlogger and Autoblogging.ai sit near $1.20 to $2, and Outrank and DispatchSEO run about $2 to $3.80, with DispatchSEO also publishing two articles a month free. The writing model costs cents everywhere; the price is the keyword research, the checking and the publishing around it.

</Verdict>

## Why does the price per article vary six times over?

Because the tools sell different amounts of work per article. The writing model is a few cents of compute on every one of them, so the spread comes from what else runs. Arvow and RightBlogger publish up to a post a day with no fact check described on their pages. Outrank publishes 30 a month and mentions fact-checking only on its $1,399 human-edited tier. Autoblogging.ai lists a fact checker on paid plans and prices images and SERP analysis as separate credits. Each DispatchSEO article starts from a keyword with real monthly searches behind it, has every claim checked against a source, and is published for you as a pull request to your GitHub repo.

## Is cheaper per article better?

Only if every article is one you would have published anyway. Google's spam policy targets pages produced in bulk to manipulate rankings, however they were made, and thirty unchecked articles a month on guessed topics is exactly that shape. Two checked articles a month on topics with measured demand carry much less of that risk, whichever tool writes them. If you will read and fix every post yourself, the cheap-per-article tools are fine. If you will not, pay for the checking.

## FAQ

<Faq>
<Q q="What does the AI model itself cost per article?">

Cents. At current API prices a researched, checked article costs well under a dollar of model time, and a plain draft far less. The rest of the price is the pipeline, the search data, the publishing integrations, the runs that stop before publishing, and margin.

</Q>
<Q q="Why is SEObot missing a per-article figure?">

Its pricing page gives a starting price of $49 but does not publish how many articles each tier includes, so the division cannot be done honestly. If the page changes, this table will.

</Q>
<Q q="Do yearly rates change the order?">

Slightly. On yearly billing Autoblogging.ai drops to $1.20, under RightBlogger's $1.63, and DispatchSEO Growth to $1.93. Arvow stays cheapest per article at 59 cents, and DispatchSEO Starter stays the most per article at $3.00, with Outrank's $2.78 just below it.

</Q>
<Q q="Does the free plan have a catch?">

DispatchSEO's free articles carry a small DispatchSEO badge, and it works only with sites that live in a GitHub repo. Autoblogging.ai's free credits buy plain drafts, without its fact checker, that you publish through your own WordPress connection. Neither asks for a card.

</Q>
